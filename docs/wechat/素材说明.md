## 01-cover.png

> Use case: ads-marketing. Create a polished editorial cover illustration for a Chinese WeChat article about the author's P2P VPN project. Landscape 2.35:1 composition suitable for a wide article cover, high resolution. Dark midnight navy background, quiet technical grid, refined cyan and teal light, restrained warm orange accents. A home NAS, an office desktop and a small ARM edge computer on three subtly floating physical platforms, connected to each other by clean luminous direct lines; a small separate cloud above suggests authentication coordination, not the central traffic hub. Sophisticated minimal isometric product illustration, crisp details, ample breathing room, no stock hacker clichés. Text must be exactly: large headline “异地组网，一条命令” and small label “P2P VPN”. The main readable title and essential devices should stay in the central safe area for cropping. No other labels, logos, watermarks, QR codes, fake screenshots or unsupported performance promises. This is an illustrative cover, not an architecture specification.
